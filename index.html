Features

Built for Clean, Readable Intel

Every module is tuned for DayZ's Chernarus and Livonia maps so nothing clutters your screen while you loot.

👤

Player ESP

Draws accurate bounding boxes around every survivor in range, with faction colouring so you instantly tell friendly from hostile.

🧟

Zombie ESP

Highlights infected with clean boxes and distance tags, letting you route around hordes instead of walking into a swarm.

💀

Skeleton Boxes

DX11-rendered skeleton tracking shows body orientation and stance, so you know exactly where a player is aiming before they peek.

📦

Loot Radar

Surfaces weapons, ammo, medical supplies and high-tier gear through walls with configurable filters and item labels.

📏

Distance Readouts

Live metre counters next to every entity keep your engagement decisions sharp and reveal how close a threat really is.

⚡

External & Lightweight

Runs as a separate process over the game window — minimal footprint, no injected DLLs, and a smooth overlay that won't tank your FPS.
